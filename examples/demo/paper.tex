\section{Introduction}
Caching remains a dominant factor in serving latency. We present FastCache,
which improves end-to-end throughput by 12.7\% across 5 seeds without
sacrificing tail latency. % see results/summary.json

\section{Evaluation}
FastCache improves end-to-end throughput by 12.7\% over the tuned baseline.
Mean request latency drops from $4.87 \pm 0.51$\,ms to $3.21 \pm 0.44$\,ms,
with an average cache hit rate of 94.2\% across 5 seeds.
All intervals use the 95\% confidence level.
